1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Eva (the “Bot”, “we”, “us”), operated by Vega, collects, uses, and protects information when you add the Bot to a Discord server or use its commands. It is meant to follow the Discord Developer Terms of Service and Developer Policy. By using the Bot, you agree to the practices described here. This policy works together with our Terms of Service.

2. What we collect

We only collect what the Bot needs to work. Depending on the features used, this can include:

  • Server data: server (guild) IDs, and the channel and role IDs you choose for features such as welcome messages, auto-roles, and log channels.
  • User data: Discord user IDs, and your username when it is shown in logs or embeds.
  • Feature data: economy and game progress, such as balances, items, cooldowns, and statistics.
  • Moderation records: actions taken with moderation commands, such as warnings, the moderator’s ID, the target’s ID, the reason given, and the time.
  • Command input: the options you type into a command, only for as long as needed to run it.

3. What we don’t collect

We do not collect passwords, email addresses, phone numbers, payment details, voice data, or private messages. The Bot does not record or store the general content of conversations in your server. We do not track your activity outside the Bot.

4. How we use data

  • to run the Bot’s commands and features (for example, keeping your balance or applying an auto-role);
  • to keep servers safe, through moderation records that server staff can review;
  • to prevent abuse and fix bugs;
  • to comply with the law and Discord’s rules.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

5. Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties, except in these cases:

  • Discord: the Bot runs on Discord, which has its own Privacy Policy. We don’t control how Discord handles data.
  • Hosting: our hosting provider stores and runs the Bot’s database on our behalf.
  • Server staff: moderation records and logs are visible to the staff of the server where the action took place.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law or to protect people from serious harm.

6. Retention

Server settings and feature data are kept while the Bot stays in your server. If the Bot is removed, we may keep server data for a short period in case you re-add it, and we delete it on request or when we clean up. User data is kept as long as it is needed for the features above, or until you ask us to delete it. Moderation records may be kept for as long as the server needs them for fair enforcement.

7. Security

We protect data with reasonable measures, including limited access to the database and private storage of the Bot’s token and credentials. No online service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you as required by law.

8. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • access the data we hold about your user ID or server;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your data;
  • stop certain uses, where the law allows.

Depending on your country (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have additional rights, and we will respect them.

9. Deleting your data

To have your data deleted, email vegavegaying@gmail.com or message us in our support server with your Discord user ID (or server ID, for server owners). We will delete the data within 30 days and confirm when it is done. Server owners can also remove the Bot at any time to stop further collection. We may keep limited information if the law requires it or if it is needed to prevent abuse, such as a block list.

10. Children

The Bot is not intended for anyone under 13, or under the minimum age required by Discord in their country. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child’s data has been collected, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date shows the latest version, and we will announce significant changes in our support server. Continuing to use the Bot after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

Questions, data requests, or complaints: vegavegaying@gmail.com or our support server.
